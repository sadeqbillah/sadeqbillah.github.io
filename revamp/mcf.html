Metal Crack Detection System

The Metal Crack Detection System is a patented solution designed to identify micro‑level defects in hydrogen fuel cell separators. These separators have shiny, patterned, and uneven surfaces that make traditional lighting and inspection methods ineffective. By innovating with upward light projection in a darkroom enclosure and a multi‑camera rig, the system achieves highly accurate defect detection in under 30 seconds per separator.

Metal Crack Detection Hydrogen Fuel Cell Separators Industrial Inspection Multi‑Camera Rig Upward Lighting Technique Motion Table Scanning Computer Vision OpenCV C#

Project Description

We engineered a novel inspection pipeline that overcame the challenges posed by shiny, zigzag patterned metal surfaces. Instead of conventional lighting, we projected light upward from beneath the separator in a closed darkroom enclosure. This allowed holes or cracks to be clearly visible to cameras positioned below. To ensure comprehensive coverage, we deployed a five‑camera rig: one central camera for frontal inspection and four angled at 45° inward to detect defects invisible from a straight view.

The system incorporated a motion tableto slide the separator in x‑y coordinates, scanning the entire surface patch by patch. Each patch was analyzed at the image level to localize defects. This approach enabled detection of micro‑holes and cracks with high precision. The inspection process was optimized to complete within 30 seconds per separator, making it suitable for industrial deployment.

Key Features Delivered:
  • Upward light projection in darkroom enclosure
  • Five‑camera rig (1 frontal + 4 angled)
  • Motion table for x‑y scanning
  • Patch‑based defect localization
  • Micro‑level crack and hole detection
  • 30‑second inspection cycle per separator

The project resulted in a patent for the innovative inspection methodology. It became a cornerstone technology for hydrogen fuel cell separator quality assurance, recognized for its speed and accuracy. The system was validated in industrial environments and certified for commercialization, ensuring reliable defect detection in production lines.
