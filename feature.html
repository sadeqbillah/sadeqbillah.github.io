Feature Phone Commercialization

Strategic commercialization of Samsung's feature phone lineup in MEA, SEA markets.

Feature Phone Commercialization Operator Customization Regional Applications Dual‑SIM QA & Production Market Support Global Fixes

Project Description

We managed the end‑to‑end commercialization process for Samsung feature phone models, collaborating closely with operators, QA teams, marketing, and production departments. The work ensured that each phone variant met regional requirements and operator specifications while maintaining Samsung’s global quality standards.

  • Led the customization and testing of feature phone models for MEA and SEA markets, ensuring compliance with operator requirements and regional regulations.
  • Single‑SIM and Dual‑SIM versions of the phone
  • Development of additional regional applications as requested by customers
  • Delivery of online updates and fixes for critical issues
  • Implementation of critical features based on operator and market needs
  • Ongoing support for models released in the market, including user‑reported issues and global fixes

Tools & Technologies

The commercialization framework was built on Samsung’s feature phone platform, leveraging C‑based development and operator integration workflows. The process combined RTOS customization, regional application development, and collaboration with cross‑functional teams to ensure smooth production rollout. The architecture emphasized scalability, maintainability, and rapid adaptation to diverse operator requirements across multiple regions.
