Door Trim Inspection System

An AI‑powered quality control solution for Hyundai Motors, this system leverages deep learning and computer vision to inspect automotive door trims with exceptional speed and accuracy. Unlike conventional setups requiring 16 cameras, our design achieves superior results with only 3 cameras, reducing inspection time by 90 seconds per trim. Integrated with

Hyundai Motors OpenCV Cognex Vision Pro, Cognex Deep Learning

Project Description

We engineered a deep learning–powered door trim inspection system that dramatically outperforms conventional solutions in both speed and efficiency. While competitors rely on 16 cameras, our design achieves superior accuracy with only 3 cameras, reducing hardware complexity and inspection time by 120 seconds per trim. This translates to a 90‑second savings per door trim, delivering measurable productivity gains on the assembly line.

The system integrates Cognex Vision Plus, Cognex Deep Learning, and custom OpenCV algorithms to detect critical assembly features, including:

  • Screw bolting
  • Plug bolt melting
  • Leadwire connections
  • Padding placement
  • Speaker model verification
  • Option Buttons Available in the Door
  • Trim color correctness

All algorithms are scalable and configurable, allowing users to adjust parameters directly through the software interface. The platform supports all door trim variations and enables multi‑recipe management, meaning new car models or design changes can be accommodated simply by selecting the appropriate recipe. This flexibility ensures seamless adaptation to evolving production requirements without costly reconfiguration.

By combining computer vision, deep learning, and smart inspection mechanisms, the system delivers a robust, future‑proof solution that reduces hardware needs, accelerates inspection cycles, and enhances quality assurance for automotive manufacturing.
